# DEMONTEKS — VEO 3.1 Fast Video Script
## Cinematic Cutscenes: Full Production Script

---

### HOW TO USE THIS SCRIPT

Each **Scene** is divided into **Clips** of ~8 seconds each, giving a minimum of 24 seconds per scene.
Paste each clip prompt individually into VEO 3.1 Fast. Upload the corresponding **Ingredient Images** (character and background assets from your game) alongside each prompt to anchor visual consistency.

**Global Style Directive** *(prepend to every prompt or set as style seed):*
> *Stylized 2D animated cinematic, vibrant pixel-art inspired color palette, bold outlines, expressive character animation, side-scrolling game aesthetic. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Dramatic lighting. No photorealism.*

## SCENE 1 — "A Strange New World"
## Plays BEFORE LEVEL 1
## ═══════════════════════════════════════

**Narrative:** The explorer wakes up on an alien planet, realizes where he is, and encounters the Demonteks for the first time — triggering the chase.

**Ingredient Images to Upload:**
- Explorer character asset
- Jungle background asset
- Demontek character asset

---

### CLIP 1 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: Alien jungle floor. Massive roots and oversized ferns. Violet sky visible between giant treetops. Dense humid mist.]**

> A rugged male explorer in a worn khaki shirt, rolled sleeves, belt with a sheathed knife, lies unconscious on the mossy alien ground. Extreme close-up on his face — eyelids flutter. His eyes open slowly, pupils contracting. He blinks, confused. He groans and sits upright, one hand pressing against the alien soil. The camera pulls back to a wide shot as he staggers to his feet, surrounded by towering alien ferns twice his height. Violet bioluminescent spores drift lazily in the air around him. Muted, eerie ambient sound. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, vibrant pixel-art inspired color palette, bold outlines, expressive character animation. Dramatic low-angle lighting, violet-tinted sky overhead.

---

### CLIP 2 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: Same jungle floor, transitioning upward toward the canopy platforms.]**

> The explorer stands in the alien jungle, slowly turning 360 degrees as the camera orbits him. He sees colossal tree trunks wider than buildings, luminous purple-leafed canopy far above, alien birds with multiple wings gliding silently between branches. His expression shifts from confusion to wide-eyed realization — this is not Earth. He looks down at his knife, then back up at the alien sky. He exhales a slow breath of disbelief. A strange "tek… tek… tek…" sound begins echoing softly in the distance. The explorer tilts his head, squinting toward the source. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, vibrant pixel-art inspired color palette, bold outlines.

---

### CLIP 3 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: A clearing between giant roots. Three small reddish creatures emerge from behind oversized leaves.]**

> Three small Demonteks — red-skinned, reptilian-demon creatures barely knee-height, with tiny horns and beady glowing eyes — waddle into view from behind a giant root. They stare up at the explorer and chant in unison: "Tek! Tek! Tek!" The explorer stares down at them for a beat, then cracks a wide grin. He bends at the waist, hands on knees, laughing — pointing at them as if mocking their tiny stature. Close-up on the Demonteks' faces: their expressions slowly shift from neutral to offended, to furious. Their eyes glow bright red. Veins pulse on their foreheads. Their tiny fists clench. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, pixel-art inspired, bold outlines, expressive character animation.

---

### CLIP 4 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: Jungle floor and lower canopy platforms. The scene bursts into chaotic energy.]**

> Suddenly dozens of Demonteks pour out from every direction — from behind roots, dropping from branches, swarming out of the undergrowth — all screeching "TEK! TEK! TEK!" in a furious red mob. The explorer's grin vanishes. His eyes go wide. He spins on his heel and sprints toward the massive tree trunks. He leaps, grabbing a dangling vine, swings up onto the first wooden platform in the canopy — a wide tree branch. He lands, rolls, looks back — the horde is already climbing after him, clinging to bark and leaping between branches. The camera pulls back to a dramatic wide shot: one lone explorer fleeing across canopy platforms against a sea of tiny raging red demons beneath a violet alien sky. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, vibrant pixel-art palette, bold outlines, high-energy kinetic motion.

## SCENE 2 — "Into the Jaws of Hell"
## Plays BEFORE LEVEL 2
## ═══════════════════════════════════════

**Narrative:** The explorer reaches the ruins but is captured by Demonteks. He wakes in a hellish underground realm, meets the King, and is thrown in the dungeon where a prisoner Demontek shows him a way out.

**Ingredient Images to Upload:**
- Explorer character asset
- Demontek character asset
- Guard Demontek character asset
- King Demontek character asset
- Dungeon background asset

---

### CLIP 1 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: Ancient mossy stone ruins at the edge of the jungle. Twilight. Crumbling pillars and collapsed archways.]**

> The explorer limps into a ruined stone courtyard, exhausted, hands on knees, breathing hard. He glances behind him — the jungle is dark and still. He sighs with relief, straightening up and scanning the ruins cautiously. He moves forward, stepping between crumbling stone columns. Suddenly — torchlight flickers on. Then another. Then another. Demontek guards in small military caps and belted uniforms step out from behind every pillar and archway, surrounding him in a tightening circle. The explorer slowly raises his hands. A net drops from above, entangling him. He struggles, stumbles, falls. Fade to black. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, pixel-art inspired palette, dramatic torch lighting, bold outlines.

---

### CLIP 2 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: A hellish underground cavern. Everything is bathed in red and orange light. Lava cracks glow in the stone floor. Fire flickers everywhere.]**

> Slow fade in from black. Close-up on the explorer's face — eyes closed, expression pained. His eyes snap open. He gasps, sitting upright. The camera pulls back to reveal he is sitting on a scorched stone floor in a massive underground realm glowing entirely in reds and oranges — cracked earth with lava seeping through fissures, flame pillars, jagged red rock formations. Countless Demonteks mill about in every direction. The explorer scrambles to his feet, spinning in disbelief. He whispers: "What… is this place?" In the distance, looming above everything else, stands a colossal reddish-brick castle. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, hellish red and orange color palette, pixel-art inspired, bold outlines, dramatic underlighting.

---

### CLIP 3 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: The castle terrace, high above. A throne carved from dark stone. Burning braziers on either side.]**

> Slow upward pan along the face of the massive reddish castle — up, up, up — until it reaches the open terrace at the top. Standing there, enormous against the fiery underground sky, is the King Demontek: a giant, broad-shouldered red demon with a long matted beard and a jagged iron crown, glaring down with glowing golden eyes. Two Guard Demonteks march the explorer forward to face him. The King studies the human with cold contempt, then speaks in a guttural alien language — "Tek'varr. Tek'dunna." — gesturing dismissively toward a trapdoor in the ground. The Guards drag the explorer away. The King turns his back. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, ominous dark-red lighting, pixel-art inspired, bold outlines, imposing scale contrast between King and explorer.

---

### CLIP 4 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: Stone dungeon. Iron-barred cells. Dim torchlight. The sound of dripping water.]**

> The explorer is thrown into a dark stone cell. He hits the ground, rolls, and sits up against the wall, catching his breath. He looks around the dungeon — other cells are visible, and inside them, small Demonteks sit imprisoned, quiet and sullen. One Demontek in the adjacent cell locks eyes with him. After a beat, it rises and approaches the bars. It gestures urgently — pointing at the floor of the explorer's cell, miming digging, then pointing further underground, then making a "dangerous" gesture (slashing across throat with claw). The explorer leans forward, studying the gestures. Slowly, he nods — he understands. He looks at the stone floor. A faint crack is visible. Close-up on his determined eyes. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, dim cool dungeon lighting, pixel-art inspired, bold outlines, tense quiet atmosphere.

## SCENE 3 — "Ancient Secrets, New Purpose"
## Plays BEFORE LEVEL 3
## ═══════════════════════════════════════

**Narrative:** The explorer escapes through the tunnel, finds an ancient ruin with weapons and wall writings that reveal the truth about the Demonteks — and decides to fight his way to the King.

**Ingredient Images to Upload:**
- Explorer character asset
- Dungeon / tunnel background asset
- Courtyard background asset

---

### CLIP 1 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: A crumbling stone chamber accessed through a hidden passage. Ancient torches magically still burning. Stone walls covered in alien pictographs and carved script.]**

> The explorer crawls through a narrow stone tunnel opening and emerges, dusty and scraped, into a hidden chamber. He stands slowly, brushing rubble from his clothes. Ancient weapons are mounted on the walls: stone-handled blades, alien sabers with glowing runes, and a long serrated sword with a crystalline edge. He approaches one wall and runs a hand along a carved alien pictograph — a carving shows a portal surrounded by humanoid figures and small demon figures. His brow furrows. He leans in closer. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, warm amber torchlight, mysterious atmosphere, pixel-art inspired palette, bold outlines.

---

### CLIP 2 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: Same hidden chamber. The explorer moves along the wall of carvings.]**

> The explorer walks slowly along the wall of ancient carvings, his torch casting long shadows. Close-up on the pictographs as he studies them — images of Demonteks living underground in prehistory, hidden from the surface world; then images of a portal being built; then images of humans appearing through the portal; then images of conflict. The explorer steps back, connecting the story. His expression hardens — this civilization has been here since the beginning, biding its time. He turns and looks at the mounted weapons. He reaches out and takes the alien saber from the wall, tests its weight in his hand. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, dim torchlight with dramatic shadows, pixel-art inspired, bold outlines, contemplative mood.

---

### CLIP 3 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: The hidden chamber, near the exit. Faint light from the hellish realm glows through a crack in the stone ahead.]**

> The explorer clips the alien saber to his belt alongside his knife. He rolls his shoulders, sets his jaw, and moves toward the crack of red light ahead. He pauses at the edge of the darkness, one hand resting on the doorframe. The camera shifts outside — through the opening, far in the distance, the King's castle looms against the fiery underground sky. The explorer stares at it with calm, cold resolve. Close-up on his eyes — no more fear, only determination. He steps forward, disappearing into the red-lit world. The camera lingers on the empty doorway as his silhouette shrinks toward the castle. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, stark contrast between cool chamber and fiery red outside world, pixel-art inspired, bold outlines, heroic tone.

## SCENE 4 — "The Last 'Tek'"
## Plays AFTER LEVEL 3 — GAME ENDING
## ═══════════════════════════════════════

**Narrative:** The explorer defeats the King Demontek. A portal opens. He escapes back to Earth — but a final warning is delivered: this is only the beginning.

**Ingredient Images to Upload:**
- Explorer character asset
- King Demontek character asset
- Castle terrace background asset
- Jungle background asset (for Earth arrival)

---

### CLIP 1 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: The castle terrace. Burning braziers. The King Demontek on his knees, defeated.]**

> The castle terrace. The explorer stands over the fallen King Demontek, who kneels with one arm braced against the stone floor, crown cracked and tilted, breathing heavily. The explorer's alien saber glows at his side. The King looks up at the explorer with an expression not of fear — but of cold warning. He speaks in his guttural language: one final utterance — "Tek… demnora… tek'shal…" then collapses fully to the ground. At that instant, a deep rumbling begins. The ground trembles. The braziers flare violently. Behind the King's fallen body, the air begins to split — a glowing tear of light appears, pulsing and expanding: a portal. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, dramatic fiery lighting, pixel-art inspired, bold outlines, epic cinematic tension.

---

### CLIP 2 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: Castle terrace. The portal fully open — swirling with white-green light. The sounds of chaos below as Demonteks react.]**

> The portal expands into a full circular gateway — swirling with a white and pale-green vortex. Through its center, another world is faintly visible: green trees, blue sky, Earth. Far below the terrace, the sound of hundreds of Demonteks screaming "TEK! TEK! TEK!" grows louder — they are coming. The explorer looks back at the chaos below, then at the portal. He takes one step toward it — then stops. He looks back one final time at the fallen King, the cracked crown, the red hellish realm stretching out beneath him. A beat of silence under the chaos. Then he turns back to the portal and runs. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, high contrast between cool portal light and hellish red background, pixel-art inspired, bold outlines, urgent pacing.

---

### CLIP 3 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: The portal threshold — half fiery castle terrace, half bright alien jungle on Earth. The moment of crossing.]**

> Slow-motion shot: the explorer leaps through the portal, his body caught mid-flight between two worlds — behind him, the hellish red underground realm with the crumbling castle; ahead of him, the dappled green light of an alien jungle with violet sky. As he passes through, the portal crackles violently. The moment his feet touch the other side, the portal collapses inward with a thunderous implosion of light — casting a shockwave that knocks him forward. He tumbles onto soft alien moss, rolls, and comes to rest face-up. He stares at the violet sky overhead. Silence. He is alive. Stylized 2D animated cinematic, split-frame lighting contrast, pixel-art inspired, bold outlines, explosive yet cathartic.

---

### CLIP 4 — Duration: ~8 seconds

**[ENVIRONMENT: The alien jungle. Quiet. The explorer on the ground, looking up at the violet sky.]**

> The explorer lies on the alien jungle floor, chest heaving, staring at the violet sky as alien spores drift around him in the silence. He laughs — a short, exhausted, disbelieving laugh. He sits up slowly, running a hand through his hair. He looks at the spot where the portal was — nothing remains but a faint scorch mark on the mossy ground. He allows himself one small smile of relief. Then — a sound. Faint, distant. But unmistakable: "…tek… tek… tek…" The explorer's smile fades. His jaw tightens. He looks at the alien saber still in his hand. The camera slowly zooms in on his eyes — then cuts to black. A single line of text appears: **"This is only the beginning."** Stylized 2D animated cinematic, quiet tension, pixel-art inspired palette, bold outlines, ominous final tone.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Continuity Checklist
| Element | Consistency Rule |
|---|---|
| Explorer | Khaki shirt, dark belt, knife always on right hip, saber acquired in Scene 3 and retained in Scene 4 |
| Demonteks | Always reddish, reptilian, small — never taller than explorer's knee |
| King Demontek | Must appear visually larger than all other Demonteks; beard + iron crown always present |
| Color World Logic | Alien jungle = violet sky + green/teal tones; Underground realm = exclusively red/orange; Earth = warm green/blue |
| Portal | Consistent white-green swirling circular vortex every time it appears |

### Clip Sequencing Per Scene
- Scene 1: Clips 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 (32 seconds)
- Scene 2: Clips 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 (32 seconds)
- Scene 3: Clips 1 → 2 → 3 (24 seconds)
- Scene 4: Clips 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 (32 seconds)

### Ingredient Image Usage
Upload the relevant game character sprite and background asset for each clip. VEO 3.1 will use these as visual anchors. If a character asset is a pixel sprite, append to your prompt:
> *"Visual style consistent with provided reference character. Maintain proportions and color palette from reference image."*
